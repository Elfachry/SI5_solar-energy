Estimate methodology

☼
Solar resource

Production is approximated using a location-based daily solar yield assumption and a system performance factor.

▦
System capacity

Panel output in watts peak is multiplied by the number of installed panels.

↗
Savings and payback

Annual energy is multiplied by the electricity rate. Payback is system cost divided by estimated annual savings.

This is an illustrative estimate, not a site survey or a guaranteed energy forecast. Actual output depends on shading, roof direction, tilt, weather and system losses.
